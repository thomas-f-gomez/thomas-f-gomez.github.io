I'm Thomas, a Data Engineer at Accenture. I started my career in logistics — supervising information flows and package traceability — which taught me something you can't learn in a textbook: data matters for the decisions it enables, not the sheer volume you accumulate.

Today, I design and operate end-to-end data pipelines, maintain cloud infrastructure using IaC, and build AI agents — all within the GCP ecosystem. I study Information Systems Engineering at UTN and continually expand my expertise across cloud, AI, and data engineering.

My philosophy: understand the business problem with absolute clarity first, then engineer the robust technical system to solve it.
